Top Japanese title: 「命令の報告は“人＋to”」

Central mechanism: quoted imperative enters a transformation gate.
COMMAND → reporting verb + person + to + base verb
NEGATIVE COMMAND → reporting verb + person + not to + base verb

Original examples:
• “Return before sunset,” Mom said. → Mom told me to return before sunset. 「帰るように言った」
• “Don’t touch the red switch,” the engineer said. → The engineer warned us not to touch the red switch. 「触らないよう警告」
• “Keep going—you can do it!” my teammates said. → My teammates encouraged me to keep going. 「続けるよう励ました」
• “Review these notes tonight,” our tutor said. → Our tutor advised us to review the notes that night. 「復習するよう助言」
