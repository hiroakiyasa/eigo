Top Japanese title: 「理由の後ろが文か名詞か」

Central sorter:
because + subject + verb clause;
because of + noun/-ing phrase;
due to / owing to = formal causal phrase;
thanks to = beneficial cause, sometimes ironic;
on account of = detailed/problematic cause.

Original examples:
• The flight was delayed because the fog was thick. 「文」
• The flight was delayed because of thick fog. 「名詞句」
• The event was canceled due to a power failure. 「公的な原因説明」
• Thanks to your directions, we arrived on time. 「好ましい原因」
• The road closed on account of flooding. 「問題の原因」

Show conversion arrow: because the road was icy ↔ because of the icy road. Note due to style can vary, but phrase after it is noun-like.

Mini practice: correct “because of the road was icy.” Answer: “because the road was icy” or “because of the icy road.” Reason: 「because ofの後ろは名詞句」.

Takeaway: 「後ろが文ならbecause、名詞ならbecause of系」.
