Top Japanese title: 「前置詞句は置き場所で働きが決まる」

Central mechanism: build “preposition + object” as one movable block. Show three placements and functions.

1 After a clause/action = adds circumstance:
• We practiced in the library. 「どこで練習したか」
• She opened the box with a coin. 「何を使ったか」

2 After a noun = explains which noun:
• The students in our club raised money. 「どの生徒かを後ろから説明」

3 Before a noun in a fixed compound-like modifier = classifies/limits:
• an under-the-seat bag 「座席下用のバッグ」
• an after-school meeting 「放課後の会議」
Explain that hyphens often bind multiword modifiers before a noun.

Object variety staircase: a preposition can take noun-like material, not only a simple noun.
• from bad to worse 「悪い状態からさらに悪く」 adjective ideas used nominally
• by checking the map 「地図を確認することで」 -ing clause
• depends on what we decide 「何を決めるかに」 wh-clause
• from under the table 「テーブルの下から」 nested phrase

Mini practice: 「“with a flashlight” は何を説明？ We searched the cave with a flashlight.」 Answer: searched; reason 「文末で動作の手段を足している」.
