Top Japanese title: 「条件の範囲を広げる・絞る」

Central condition-range map:
even if = result unchanged even at an extreme condition;
if/whether after verbs = whether A is true, not a condition;
only if = necessary condition;
unless = except if / outside the “not” condition range;
provided/providing that = strict permission condition.

Original examples:
• Even if we lose, I'll be proud of the team. 「負けても結論不変」
• I don't know if the shop is open. 「開いているかどうか」
• You may enter only if you show your pass. 「入場の必要条件」
• We won't leave unless the fog clears. 「霧が晴れない限り」
• You can borrow it provided that you return it today. 「厳密な条件」

Mini practice: choose unless/if: “Don't call me ___ it is urgent.” Answer: unless; reason 「緊急の場合を除いて電話しない」.

Takeaway: 「条件語は、結果が有効になる範囲を設計する」.
