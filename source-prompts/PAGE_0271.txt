Top Japanese title: 「時制は話し手の視点で選ぶ」

Teach that tense is not a mechanical calendar label: it locates an event from the speaker's chosen viewpoint. Future time/condition clauses normally use present form; present perfect can mark completion before the future main event; will appears when willingness/prediction itself matters. Historical present can bring a past story to life.

Original examples:
• I'll call you when I check the schedule. 「確認する時点を前提化」
• I'll call you when I've checked the schedule. 「確認完了後を強調」
• If the weather improves, we will leave. 「if節は未来でも現在形」
• Yesterday I open the gate, and a huge dog runs toward me! 「実況的な歴史的現在」

Central camera/timeline diagram: ordinary present, vivid story-now, and future viewpoint. Show meaning difference between check and have checked.

Takeaway: 「時間だけでなく、どこから出来事を見るかを選ぶ」.
