Top Japanese title: 「for・sinceと『だから今』」

Central split mechanism:
FOR = length/duration;
SINCE = starting point;
RESULT present perfect = past cause whose current consequence matters.

Original examples:
• I've worked here for six months. 「6か月という長さ」
• I've worked here since February. 「2月という起点」
• She has been awake for three hours. 「継続時間」
• I've lost my key, so I can't get inside. 「過去の紛失→今入れない」
• The road has frozen, so driving is dangerous now. 「現在の結果」
