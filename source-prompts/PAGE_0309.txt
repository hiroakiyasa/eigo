Top Japanese title: 「理由を結ぶbecause・as・since」

Central reason-strength bridge: because clearly answers why and focuses the cause; as/since often present a lighter or assumed background reason. Add conversational frames “Because...” as an answer and “That's because...”. Include formal in that = in the respect that; for as literary/formal afterthought.

Original examples:
• We stayed inside because the wind was fierce. 「理由を明確に焦点化」
• As the road was icy, classes started late. 「背景理由」
• Since everyone is ready, let's begin. 「共有済みの理由」
• The device is useful in that it works without electricity. 「その点において」
• Why did you leave? — Because I felt ill. 「直接の返答」

Mini practice: which best directly answers “Why?”—because/as? Answer: because; reason 「原因を最も明確に提示」.

Takeaway: 「理由を強く焦点化するか、背景として添えるかを選ぶ」.
