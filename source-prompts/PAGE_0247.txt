Top Japanese title: 「whatは『もの・こと』を包む」

Teach fused/free relative what as both the guide and the missing noun: “what + clause” = the thing(s) that.... Contrast with which + noun/known choices. Then show whoever/whatever/whichever as an open-choice package; depending on context it means any choice or no matter which.

Original examples:
• What you created impressed the judges. 「あなたが作ったもの」
• Tell me what happened. 「何が起きたか」
• Take whatever you need. 「必要なものは何でも」
• Choose whichever route feels safest. 「どちらの道でも、最も安全な方」
• Whoever calls, take a message. 「誰が電話してきても」

Central formula: what = the thing(s) that; wh-ever wheel opens every candidate. Avoid adding an extra noun immediately before fused what.

Mini practice: translate “Whatever happens, stay calm.” Answer: 「何が起きても落ち着いて」; reason 「everが選択・条件を全面的に開く」.

Takeaway: 「whatは空所と『もの』を一語でまとめる」.
