Top Japanese title: 「進行形の不満から現在完了へ」

Top mechanism: always/constantly/forever + progressive can express repeated behavior colored by irritation, surprise, or affection—not neutral frequency alone.
• Kai is always leaving cups on my desk. 「いつも置きっぱなし！」
• You're constantly changing the plan. 「また変えている」

Bottom mechanism introduces present perfect: have/has + past participle brings a past event/state into the speaker's present perspective.
• I've completed the first draft. 「今、完成済み」
• She has broken her glasses. 「今困る結果がある」

Show form control: have/has moves before the subject in questions and takes not in negatives: Have you finished? / I haven't finished.

Mini practice: make a question from “Mina has packed the bags.” Answer: “Has Mina packed the bags?” Reason: 「have/hasが時を担う助動詞」.

Takeaway: 「進行形は感情を映し、完了形は過去を今へ引き寄せる」.
