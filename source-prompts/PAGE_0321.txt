Upper mechanism 1: NOUN + content clause. Show an empty “content” drawer behind an abstract noun, filled by a that-clause.
• We heard a rumor that the old bridge would reopen. 「rumorの具体的内容」
• I had a feeling that someone was following us. 「feelingの中身」
• They reached the conclusion that the map was outdated. 「conclusionの内容」
Explain: nouns such as rumor, belief, feeling, idea, hope, and conclusion invite a following clause that tells what the content is. Distinguish this from a relative clause: the clause supplies content, not merely identifies a noun.
