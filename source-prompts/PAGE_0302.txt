Top Japanese title: 「andは前へ進む流れ」

Teach that and does more than add: order and context can create time sequence, consequence, and continuing repetition. Imperative + and means “do this and then/therefore...”. SO explicitly points from cause to result.

Original examples:
• Mia opened the chest and found a silver key. 「動作が順に進む」
• Press this button, and the door will open. 「そうすれば」
• The child asked and asked, so I finally agreed. 「反復→結果」
• It was getting dark, so we turned back. 「理由から結果へ」

Central flow diagram: A → AND → B; repeated AND loop; cause → SO → consequence. Contrast simple addition with logical result labels.

Mini practice: what does and mean in “Study steadily, and you'll improve”? Answer: 「そうすれば」; reason 「命令・助言の実行が後半の条件になる」.

Takeaway: 「andは情報を前へ運び、soは結果を明示する」.
