Top Japanese title: 「疑問文は助動詞を前へ」

Central word-order mechanism: statement → move auxiliary/be before subject; if none exists, insert do/does/did and return the main verb to base form. Show that word-order disruption signals the speaker's desire to know.

Original transformations:
• The explorers have returned. → Have the explorers returned?
• Emi likes the plan. → Does Emi like the plan?
• You found the cave. → Did you find the cave?
• The gate was open. → Was the gate open?
