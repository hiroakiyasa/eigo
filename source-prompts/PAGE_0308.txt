Top Japanese title: 「条件に備える表現の道具箱」

Organize conditions by mental job:
given (that) = consider a known fact;
as long as = within a continuing condition;
suppose/supposing = imagine a premise;
in case = take action now as preparation;
otherwise = if not / different outcome;
for fear that / lest = formal prevention.

Original examples:
• Given the weather, the delay is understandable. 「事情を考慮」
• You can stay as long as you keep the room tidy. 「条件が続く範囲」
• Suppose the battery fails—what will we do? 「仮の前提」
• Take some water in case the shops are closed. 「事前の備え」
• Keep this receipt; otherwise, you cannot return the item. 「そうでなければ」

Contrast in case vs if with a preparation timeline.

Mini practice: “Bring a charger ___ your phone runs low.” Answer: in case; reason 「電池切れに前もって備える」.

Takeaway: 「条件の種類に合わせて、想定・備え・別結果を選ぶ」.
