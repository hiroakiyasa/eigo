Top Japanese title: 「禁止と不要をまちがえない」

Central contrast must be unmistakable:
RED STOP: mustn't + base verb = 「してはいけない」 prohibition.
GREEN OPTIONAL: don't/doesn't have to + base verb = 「する必要はない」 no obligation; doing it is allowed.

Original examples:
• You mustn't feed the animals. 「動物に餌を与えてはいけない」
• You don't have to bring a laptop. 「持参しなくてもよい」
• Mira doesn't have to stay late. 「遅くまで残る必要はない」

Add a logic picture: mustn't closes the action path; don't have to opens both “do it” and “not do it” paths.
