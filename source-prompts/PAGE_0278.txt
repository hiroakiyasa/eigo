Top Japanese title: 「今に届く完了と経験」

Central split visual. Recent completion/news: just, already, yet around have + past participle. Experience: past events held as part of one's life up to now, often with ever/never.

Original examples:
• I've just found your notebook. 「たった今見つけた」
• She has already sent the file. 「もう送った」
• Have you finished yet? 「もう終えた？」
• Have you ever ridden a horse? 「これまでに経験がある？」
• I've never camped in winter. 「今まで一度もない」

Show typical placement: have + just/already/ever/never + past participle; yet commonly at sentence end in questions/negatives. Note that a finished past time such as yesterday usually calls for simple past.
