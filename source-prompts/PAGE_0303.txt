Top Japanese title: 「論理の道しるべを選ぶ」

Central five-branch signpost:
result/conclusion: therefore, consequently;
addition: moreover, in addition;
example: for example, for instance;
clarification: that is, in other words;
contrast turn: but, however.
Explain that many are sentence adverbs rather than simple coordinating conjunctions, so punctuation matters.

Original examples:
• The road was flooded; therefore, we turned back. 「論理的結論」
• The cabin is warm. Moreover, it has solar power. 「追加」
• The kit contains useful tools—for example, a compass. 「例示」
• We need a backup; in other words, one plan is not enough. 「言い換え」

Mini practice: choose “for example / therefore”: “Several birds migrate; ___, swallows travel long distances.” Answer: for example; reason 「具体例を挙げる」.

Takeaway: 「足す・結ぶだけでなく、情報の論理関係を標識で示す」.
