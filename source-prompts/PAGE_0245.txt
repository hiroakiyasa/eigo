Top Japanese title: 「whetherとwh節を名詞の席へ」

Central split illustration. WHETHER balance holds A / not A and can serve as subject; avoid sentence-initial if for this formal noun-clause use. WH clause machine changes a direct question into statement order inside a larger sentence.

Original examples:
• Whether the bus will come is uncertain. 「来るかどうか」
• Whether we stay or leave depends on the weather. 「残るか出るか」
• Where Ken went remains a mystery. 「Kenがどこへ行ったか」
• Our choice depends on how much time remains. 「どれほど時間が残るか」

Contrast: “Where did Ken go?” direct question → “where Ken went” embedded clause. Show wh + subject + verb, no do-support or inversion.
