Top Japanese title: 「ifは条件を仮置きする」

Teach if as temporarily accepting one condition branch, not automatically “low probability.” In ordinary future conditions, use present form in the if-clause and will/can/may/imperative in the main clause. Present or progressive main clauses can sound like firm declarations/threats.

Original examples:
• If the weather improves, we'll leave at noon. 「現実的な未来条件」
• If you see Mina, give her this note. 「条件＋命令」
• If the gate is locked, we can use the side door. 「可能な対応」
• If you touch my camera again, I'm locking it away. 「強い決意」
