Top Japanese title: 「-ingを目的語と説明に置く」

Central three-door mechanism:
1 verb + -ing as object,
2 preposition + -ing because a preposition needs noun-like material,
3 be + -ing, which can be progressive action or a noun-like description depending on meaning.

Original examples:
• Leo avoids checking his phone while studying. 「avoidsの目的語」
• Mia is good at reading old maps. 「前置詞atの目的語」
• The fox is crossing the bridge. 「進行中の動作」
• My favorite activity is sketching ruins. 「activityの内容説明」
