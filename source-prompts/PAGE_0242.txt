Top Japanese title: 「過去分詞は前で分類、後ろで説明」

Central contrast:
past participle before noun = compact label/type or established characteristic;
past participle after noun or after a verb = fuller state description, often with its own details.

Original examples:
• We followed the marked trail. 「印の付いた種類の道」
• Please return the signed form. 「署名済みの用紙」
• The letter written in blue ink was mine. 「written以下がletterを詳しく説明」
• Nora reached the cabin exhausted. 「Nora＝疲れ切った状態」
• Fallen leaves covered the steps. 「落ち終わった後の状態」

Explain that intransitive past participles such as fallen can express a completed resulting state, while not every verb works freely this way.

Mini practice: what does “written” modify in “the message written on the wall”? Answer: message; reason 「名詞の後ろから、どのmessageかを説明」.
