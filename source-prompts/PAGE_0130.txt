Top Japanese title: 「結果へつなぐ so・such・too・rather」

Central mechanism illustration: four brightly separated paths that all start with a judgment and lead to a consequence or contrast.

Path 1 SO: 「so + 形容詞/副詞 + that 結果」
• The cave was so dark that we used a lamp. 「とても暗かったのでランプを使った」

Path 2 SUCH: 「such + (a/an) + 形容詞 + 名詞 + that 結果」 and explain it points to the kind/degree of a noun phrase.
• It was such a quiet room that I heard the clock. 「とても静かな部屋だったので時計が聞こえた」

Path 3 TOO: 「too + 形容詞 + to do」 means more than suitable/possible, often an unwanted excess.
• The shelf is too high for me to reach. 「高すぎて私には届かない」

Path 4 RATHER: show “rather A than B” as preference/contrast.
• I would rather walk than wait. 「待つより歩きたい」
Also add a small badge: rather + adjective can mean 「予想より／やや」, as in “rather noisy”.

Comparison box: so hot that... / such a hot day that... / too hot to touch. Highlight different structures, not interchangeable word-for-word.

Mini practice: choose so/such: “It was ___ an exciting match that everyone stood up.” Answer: such. Reason: 「an exciting match という名詞句の前」.

Takeaway: 「形容詞なら so、名詞句なら such、限界超えなら too、選択の対比なら rather」. Dense four-path diagram, multiple original examples, concise Japanese explanations, integrated question+answer.
