Top Japanese title: 「状態は現在形、動作は進行形」

Teach that simple present often presents thought, feeling, possession, perception and declaration as states integrated with the speaker's current reality; progressive portrays a dynamic scene from outside. Avoid mechanically classifying words—meaning in context matters.

Original examples:
• I know the answer. 「知識という状態」
• I can hear music upstairs. 「自然に聞こえる」
• This bag belongs to Mina. 「所有関係」
• I apologize for the delay. 「発言と同時に成立する宣言」
• I'm listening to the music carefully. 「意識的な動作」

Contrast: hear vs listen; have possession vs having an experience where appropriate. Red correction: “I'm knowing the answer.” ✗ → “I know the answer.”

Mini practice: choose “I see / I'm seeing what you mean.” Answer: I see; reason 「理解した状態を示す標準表現」.

Takeaway: 「静かな状態は現在形、外から映せる動きは進行形」.
