Top Japanese title: 「butは予想の流れを反転する」

Teach but as a reversal of expectation, including polite apology + request and not A but B. Contrast coordinating but with sentence adverb however; show correct punctuation. Include yet for a surprising continuation and preposition but = except.

Original examples:
• The bag is small, but it holds a lot. 「予想を反転」
• Sorry, but could you move your bag? 「前置き＋依頼」
• We need courage, not luck. / not A but B 「訂正・対比」
• The task was difficult; however, we finished it. 「独立した文副詞」
• Everyone but Leo arrived. 「Leo以外」

Central punctuation map: clause, but clause / clause; however, clause. Red correction for comma splice with however.
