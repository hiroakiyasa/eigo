Critical contrast of discourse phrases:
ON THE CONTRARY = correct the previous claim with its opposite.
• “The village is deserted.” — “On the contrary, it is lively tonight.” 「正反対だと訂正」
ON THE OTHER HAND = add another side, not necessarily deny the first.
• The cabin is cheap. On the other hand, it is far from town. 「別の面では」
