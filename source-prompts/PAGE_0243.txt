Teach a past-participle phrase as a formal/compact background description whose understood subject normally matches the main-clause subject. Show movement from after the noun to sentence-front. Also clarify that a bare past participle cannot normally serve as a noun-like subject; use being/to be + past participle.

Original examples:
• Built beside the lake, the hotel offers splendid views. 「湖畔に建てられているので」
• Surrounded by fog, the boat moved slowly. 「霧に囲まれた状態で」
• Being ignored can feel painful. 「無視されること」
• I dislike being rushed. 「急かされることが嫌い」
