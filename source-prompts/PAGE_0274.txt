Top Japanese title: 「進行形は動く場面を映す」

Teach be + -ing as a subject described by an unfolding activity. BE carries tense, question and negation; -ing supplies the moving scene. Contrast simple present's habit/stability with progressive's temporary/in-progress situation.

Original examples:
• The children are building a tree house. 「今進行する場面」
• I'm staying with my aunt this month. 「一時的な滞在」
• I live near the station. 「安定した生活状況」
• Are they still waiting? / They aren't waiting now. 「beを動かして疑問・否定」

Central mechanism: subject → BE control panel → moving -ING video; side-by-side wide habit ring versus short temporary interval.

Mini practice: choose “I work / I'm working from home this week.” Answer: I'm working; reason 「this weekだけの一時的状況」.

Takeaway: 「beが時を決め、-ingが動く映像を見せる」.
