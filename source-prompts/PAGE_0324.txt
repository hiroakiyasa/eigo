Top Japanese title: 「伝達動詞の型と疑問文レポート」

Upper receiver map: choose construction by how strongly the verb requires a listener.

TYPE 1 verb + person + content; receiver is essential:
tell, inform, remind, assure
• The guide told us that the path was closed. 「tellは誰に伝えたかが必要」

TYPE 2 verb + (person) + content; receiver optional:
promise, ask
• Nao promised me that she would return the key. / Nao promised that... 「相手なしでも成立」

TYPE 3 verb + preposition + person + content:
say to, admit to, explain to, complain to; agree with
• Leo explained to me why the compass failed. 「受け手はtoで追加」
Add warning: say me ✗; tell me ✓; explain me ✗; explain to me ✓.

Lower main transformation: REPORTING A QUESTION. Show question marks entering a machine and emerging as embedded-clause word order.
Yes/no question:
• “Did you lock the cabin?” → She asked me whether/if I had locked the cabin. 「if/whether + 主語＋動詞」
WH question:
• “Where is the first-aid kit?” → He asked where the first-aid kit was. 「wh語を残し、倒置を戻す」
Rule banner: no do/does/did and no question inversion inside the reported clause.

Suggestion report:
• “Let’s leave before dawn.” → Mina suggested that we leave before dawn. / Mina suggested leaving before dawn. 「内容に合わせて自由に再構成」

Mini practice: 「報告文へ：“Why are you laughing?” she asked Leo.」 Answer: 「She asked Leo why he was laughing.」 Reason: 「wh語whyを残し、he wasの平叙文語順に戻す」.

Bottom takeaway: 「動詞の型で受け手を置き、疑問文は節の語順へ戻す」. Dense, precise, readable.
